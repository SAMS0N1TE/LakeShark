CC1101 RAW 2-FSK replay — implementation and bench summary, 2026-10-01

Flipper RAW files with stock 2FSKDev238/476Async presets or supported custom
CC1101 asynchronous 2-FSK presets can replay once through CC1101/RMT.
Preset modem timing, deviation and frequency offset are preserved; operator
power and board routing remain driver-controlled. Replay requires complete
RAW edges within supported CC1101 bands, at most 4096 edges and 10 seconds.
Malformed presets, unsupported modulation and unknown declared modules are
rejected. Existing OOK and legacy SX1262 paths remain supported.

RECORD/FILES recognize CC1101 FSK, display the correct radio and offer its
-10/0/5/10 dBm power choices. Loading a file does not transmit; PLAY is explicit.
The simulator's -r option loads a replay file for portrait/landscape/touch QA.
REC console help now accurately advertises 'rec freq <MHz>'.
