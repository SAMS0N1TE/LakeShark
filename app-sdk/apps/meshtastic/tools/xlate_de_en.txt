# Hand-written German -> English data for mt_xlate_lang (MESHTASTIC app).
# Written for this app; GPL-3.0-or-later like the rest of LakeShark.
# gen_xlate_lang.py reads it with the Wiktionary lexicon and writes ../lang/de.bin.
# The format is described at the top of xlate_pt_en.txt. Keys fold umlauts as
# ae, oe, ue and sharp s as ss (fuer, schoen); the generator also lets common
# words typed without umlauts through (fur, schon is "already" and stays so).
# The reader brings a verb at the end of the clause (weil ich da bin, habe ...
# gesehen) forward and joins a separable particle (rufe ... an) to its verb.
