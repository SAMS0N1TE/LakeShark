# Hand-written Russian -> English data for mt_xlate_lang (MESHTASTIC app).
# Written for this app; GPL-3.0-or-later like the rest of LakeShark.
# gen_xlate_lang.py reads it with the Wiktionary lexicon and writes ../lang/ru.bin.
# The format is described at the top of xlate_pt_en.txt. Keys are written in
# Cyrillic and folded to their transliteration (BGN/PCGN without marks: zh kh ts
# ch sh shch y yu ya; e for e and yo; the soft and hard signs left out), so
# a line typed in Latin ("privet, kak dela") meets the same entries. Chat
# spellings may be Latin or Cyrillic. There are no articles; a subject that is
# a pronoun with no verb gets "is" (ya doma: I am at home).
