# Hand-written Ukrainian -> English data for mt_xlate_lang (MESHTASTIC app).
# Written for this app; GPL-3.0-or-later like the rest of LakeShark.
# gen_xlate_lang.py reads it with the Wiktionary lexicon and writes ../lang/uk.bin.
# The format is described at the top of xlate_pt_en.txt. Keys are written in
# Cyrillic and folded to their transliteration as Ukrainian has it (h for г,
# g for ґ, y for и, i for і, yi for ї, ye for є; the soft sign and the
# apostrophe left out), so a line typed in Latin ("pryvit, yak spravy") meets
# the same entries. There are no articles; a subject that is a pronoun with no
# verb gets "is" (ya vdoma: I am at home).
