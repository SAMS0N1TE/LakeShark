# Hand-written Polish -> English data for mt_xlate_lang (MESHTASTIC app).
# Written for this app; GPL-3.0-or-later like the rest of LakeShark.
# gen_xlate_lang.py reads it with the Wiktionary lexicon and writes ../lang/pl.bin.
# The format is described at the top of xlate_pt_en.txt. Keys fold ą ć ę ł ń ó
# ś ź ż to plain letters (l for ł), as chat typed without them does. There
# are no articles; the subject is often left out (słyszę cię: I hear you).
